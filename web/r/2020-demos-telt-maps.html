Lyon-Turin Multimodal Network Maps

TELT needed to explain a 57km binational rail tunnel not as an isolated infrastructure project, but as part of a much larger connected network — European rail corridors, long-distance cycling routes, and Alpine hiking trails all converging on the same crossing.

Commissioned by TELT, the France-Italy public company delivering the Lyon-Turin railway, with Finnish think tank Demos Helsinki as concept intermediary and SPIN Unit producing the cartography. This 2020 mapping work later became the visual foundation of TELT’s 2023 public “SMARTLAND” storymap.

Built entirely from open European geodata — Eurostat administrative boundaries, EEA hydrography, EuroVelo cycling routes, and TEN-T rail corridor data — at three nested scales: a pan-European overview, a France-Italy regional map, and a detailed local map of the Susa/Maurienne valleys around the Mont Cenis base tunnel itself.

Delivered as a set of finished cartographic maps in November 2020, produced in QGIS.
