An international network of academics and professionals, external to the market.

SPIN Unit combines design, artistic and technical practice to find new approaches to urban analytics. Born as an academic endeavour in Tallinn in 2012, it has grown into a network of researchers and practitioners delivering studies, strategies and tools for cities, ministries and international organisations — while staying reserved for the collective projects we truly believe in.

Our method, metamorphology, reads spatial configuration, known amenities and everyday activity together: what people do in a space on a par with how the space is laid out. The repository on this site is the method's evidence — every study, with its data sources and its reports, open.

SPIN Unit develops and runs CoPlanAI, a platform for collaborative urban planning, and earlier co-created UrbanistAI.
